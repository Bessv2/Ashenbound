# The Forgotten Catacombs: second dungeon

The owner asked on 2026-10-22 to "keep going with a second dungeon". This is
the second dungeon in the Fallen Marches and the first step of the
charter's dungeon progression: a harder place that opens once the first is
beaten.

**Status:** built and playable. Numbers are first-pass tuning to be adjusted
in playtests.

## The run in one paragraph

Below the crypt, the dead of older wars were walled into catacombs that
water and time have half reclaimed. The hero descends a **Stairwell** and
crosses three combat rooms, each sealed until cleared:

- the **Gallery of Bones**, where Ghouls pour out of the niches;
- the **Drowned Vault**, three waves ending with a Fallen Knight;
- the **Bone Pit**, a tight room with a Knight and Ghoul packs.

Then comes the **Hollow Throne**, where the Hollow King wakes. Clearing it
wins the run. A full run takes about 8 to 10 minutes, a little longer than
the crypt.

## Opening it

- It opens once the Ashen Crypt has been cleared at any tier
  (`Profile.tier_cleared ≥ 1`). Before that, its button on the title screen
  reads "The Forgotten Catacombs · clear the crypt" and cannot be chosen.
- The title screen's dungeon row picks the dungeon. The choice is saved
  (`Profile.dungeon`), and the Enter button names it ("Enter the
  catacombs").
- **Tiers are shared:** the tier on the title screen applies to either
  dungeon, with the same scaling and champion chances
  ([tiers.md](tiers.md)). A win here opens the next tier too.
- **Loot:** item level 6 at tier 1, against the crypt's 2, so its drops are
  stronger.

## Layout

The same building blocks as the crypt (`CryptRoom`, `CryptGate`,
`CombatRoom`), on the 4 m grid, in a line going north. The scene is
generated by `tools/gen/catacombs.py`, so rooms, gates, markers and combat
areas stay consistent with each other. To change the layout, edit the
script's tables and re-run it.

| Room | Size | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Stairwell | 12 × 12 m | Start, no enemies. The door north opens at once. |
| Gallery of Bones | 24 × 20 m | Encounter 1. Four soul-fire pillars. |
| Drowned Vault | 20 × 24 m | Encounter 2. Four pillars, barrels by the far wall. |
| Bone Pit | 16 × 16 m | Encounter 3. Two pillars; a tight room. |
| Hollow Throne | 28 × 28 m | The boss. Four braziers, banners, the throne. |

**Look:**
- Every torch, pillar and brazier burns cold soul-fire: cyan flames and
  light, using `CryptRoom.torch_*_color` and the `FlameFx` colours.
- Its own environment (`catacombs_environment.tres`): a cooler ambient light
  and a teal fog.
- The moonlight is a little dimmer and bluer.

## Boss: the Hollow King

A giant Skeleton Mage (1.8×) in a blue-grey, softly glowing material, with
his staff and a cold light. **700 HP.** It uses the same three-phase script
as the Ashen Lord (`AshenLord`), with its own attacks and adds. The name on
the boss bar comes from `display_title`.

| Phase | Health | Moves |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The King** | 100% to 60% | **Soul Volley:** a 0.9 s windup with a 40° sector telegraph, then three soul bolts fanned across it (14 each, 11 m/s, 14 m). He casts from up to 9 m with a clear line of sight. **Grave Spikes:** when the hero is within 3.6 m, a 1.1 s windup and a 3.6 m circle, 30 damage. |
| **2. The Restless** (at 60%) | 60% to 30% | Roars (2 s, invulnerable), then raises **4 Ghouls** at the throne's corners, once. Adds **Soul Nova:** a cyan ring expanding to 11 m, 18 damage. |
| **3. Unbound** (at 30%) | 30% to 0 | Roars again: windups 25% shorter, movement 30% faster, Soul Nova every other attack. |

The Ghouls he raises die with him. Rewards: 600 XP, 120 gold, 60 Ash, and
the Ashen Lord's boss drop table (three items, Rare or better).

## Rewards

A full run at tier 1 earns **113 Ash** (the crypt earns 76), plus XP, gold
and loot at item level 6. The same "paid once" rule applies.

## Acceptance checks

These are automated in `tests/test_catacombs.gd`.

1. A Ghoul has 18 health, runs faster than a skeleton, and claws for 7.
2. The Hollow King:
   - fires three fanned bolts after his telegraph;
   - raises four Ghouls at 60%;
   - those Ghouls fall with him.
3. The catacombs have four encounters with banners, every marker their
   waves name, shut gates, and cold fire.
4. A full run wins at the Hollow Throne, pays 113 Ash once, and shows the
   catacombs' own victory title.
5. The catacombs are locked until the crypt is cleared. The choice is saved,
   and older saves play the crypt.
6. Main plays the chosen dungeon.
7. The title screen offers only the open dungeons and names the way in.
